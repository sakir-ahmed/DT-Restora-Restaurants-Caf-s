# DT Restora Facebook page: Messenger setup and reply templates

Set these in **Meta Business Suite, Inbox, Automations** (names may vary). Reply to every message within 15 minutes during business hours, because speed is the biggest factor in booking demos.

## 5. Saved replies (copy into Inbox, Saved replies)

Use a short name for each so you can insert it in two clicks.

**price**
DT Restora costs 1,000 BDT per month (regular 5,700 BDT). Installation is a one-time 2,500 BDT, refundable after one year if you cancel. First month free after installation. Yearly plan gives 2 months free. Would you like a free demo?

**demo**
Happy to show you! The quickest way is the free live demo: https://dt-restora.datatechbd.com. If you prefer, we can walk through it on a call with your menu and branches. What time suits you today or tomorrow?

**features**
DT Restora covers POS and billing, kitchen display, recipes and stock, staff attendance and payroll, loyalty and coupons, an online ordering website with QR tables, delivery and accounting. You can start with the till and kitchen and add the rest later.

**offline**
Yes, it works offline for cash orders. Menu and prices are saved on the device, receipts still print, and everything syncs when the internet returns. Online payments and SMS need internet.

**bangla**
Yes, DT Restora works in Bangla and English: screens, messages, SMS and receipts. Your team can use it in the language they prefer.

**delivery-apps**
Yes. You can enter orders from delivery apps like Foodpanda or Pathao Food on one screen, record the commission and reconcile the payout. Direct connections depend on each platform giving partner access.

**hardware**
You need a tablet, touch monitor or computer for the counter, and an 80mm thermal printer for receipts. A cash drawer and barcode scanner are optional. A screen or printer for the kitchen is recommended.

**next-step**
Great. Next step is a short demo, about 20 minutes. Please share your restaurant name, area, number of branches and the best time to call. We will confirm.

**follow-up-1 (day after demo)**
Thank you for your time yesterday. Do you have any questions about what you saw? If you would like to start, we can set an installation date this week.

**follow-up-2 (day 3)**
A quick idea from our customers: many owners start with POS and kitchen first, then add stock and the website. Would you like us to prepare your menu in a trial setup?

**not-now**
No problem at all. We will keep your details and send occasional tips. If you want a demo later, message us anytime or WhatsApp +880 1575-888775.

**thanks-review**
Thank you for trying DT Restora! If you were happy, a short review on our page helps other restaurant owners find us. We would be grateful.

## 6. Lead qualification (ask in this order, keep it friendly)

1. What is your restaurant or cafe name and area?
2. How many branches do you run?
3. What do you use for billing and stock today (notebook, Excel, another software)?
4. What is your biggest headache (waste, cash, staff, delivery apps, reports)?
5. Do you want online ordering and delivery too?
6. When is a good time for a 20-minute demo?

Record each lead in `docs/marketing/social-media-posts/campaign/lead-tracker-template.csv`.
