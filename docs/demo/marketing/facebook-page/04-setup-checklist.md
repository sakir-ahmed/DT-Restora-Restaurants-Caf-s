# DT Restora Facebook page: setup checklist

Tick each box in order. Most of this takes about two hours. Facebook changes its menus, so use the names as a guide.

## A. Before you create the page

- [ ] Log in with your personal account that will own the page (it stays private; nothing is shown).
- [ ] Decide the name and username (see `01-page-identity.md`).
- [ ] Open the folder `assets/` and have the profile picture and cover ready on your computer or phone.
- [ ] Have your address and opening hours ready, or decide to hide them.

## B. Create the page

- [ ] Create a new Page. Name: **DT Restora**. Category: **Software Company**.
- [ ] Upload **profile picture**: `assets/profile-picture-1-slate.png` (1080x1080). Facebook crops it to a circle; `assets/profile-picture-preview.png` shows how it looks.
- [ ] Upload **cover photo**: `assets/cover-1-profit.png` (1640x624). Check the mobile preview, then save.
- [ ] Set the **username** (`@dt.restora` or an alternative).
- [ ] Paste the **Intro (bio)** from `02-bio-about-story.md`.
- [ ] Paste the **Short About** and **Long About** text.
- [ ] Add **website**, **phone**, **email**, **WhatsApp**, **address** and **hours**.
- [ ] Add the **Call-to-action button** (WhatsApp or Send message).
- [ ] Add the **Services** with descriptions from `01-page-identity.md`.
- [ ] Set the **tab order** and turn on Reviews, Messenger and Services.

## C. Messenger and inbox

- [ ] Turn on Messenger. Add the **greeting**, **instant reply**, **away message** and **FAQ** from `03-messenger-and-replies.md`.
- [ ] Add the **saved replies**.
- [ ] Install the Meta Business Suite app on your phone and turn on notifications for messages.
- [ ] Decide who answers messages and when (aim for under 15 minutes in business hours).

## D. Security and roles

- [ ] Add a second **admin**.
- [ ] Turn on **two-factor authentication** for every admin.
- [ ] Save backup codes somewhere safe.
- [ ] Add your Instagram, WhatsApp Business and (if used) a Meta Business account.

## E. Content before you invite anyone

An empty page does not convince visitors. Publish these first:

- [ ] **Intro post**: `assets/intro-post-hello.png` (text in `05-launch-30-days.md`).
- [ ] **Launch offer** post and **pin it**: `assets/pinned-post-launch-offer.png` (and the Bangla version).
- [ ] Upload **9 to 12 posts** from `../social-media-posts/posts/` so the feed already looks active (see the 30-day plan).
- [ ] Create **photo albums**: "Product screenshots" (from `../product-screenshots/`), "Tips for owners", "Offers".
- [ ] Upload one short **demo video** (screen recording of the dashboard, POS and kitchen display).
- [ ] Fill the **Featured** section with the pinned offer and the demo video.

## F. Connect and promote

- [ ] Add the Facebook page link to the **website footer**, your **email signature**, **WhatsApp Business profile** and **business cards**.
- [ ] Add the page to your **Google Business Profile** and LinkedIn page.
- [ ] Update the Facebook link on the demo site if you list social links.
- [ ] Invite your personal contacts, partners and suppliers to like the page (Facebook's invite button).
- [ ] Join 5 to 10 restaurant and cafe owner groups and read their rules before posting.

## G. Compliance and tracking

- [ ] Add a **privacy policy** URL on datatechbd.com before running lead ads.
- [ ] Use the tracked links from each `post.txt` (they contain `utm_source=facebook` and a post number).
- [ ] Create a copy of `../social-media-posts/campaign/lead-tracker-template.csv` in Google Sheets for leads.
- [ ] Do not make guarantees (for example "double your profit"). Describe what the system does.
- [ ] Do not buy followers or likes. They hurt reach.

## H. Weekly routine (15 minutes a day)

1. Reply to messages and comments.
2. Post the planned post (see `05-launch-30-days.md`).
3. Share a story if you have a clip.
4. Note leads in the tracker.
5. On Sunday check: messages received, demos booked, best posts.
