Part 1 · Why

Your restaurant is losing money in places you cannot see.

Busy kitchens rarely fail because the food is bad. They struggle because small leaks go unnoticed for months.

Stock that is a guess

Delivery apps on separate tablets

You cannot manage what you cannot see. DT Restora turns each of these blind spots into a number on your screen.

Questions owners ask
Good to know

Short answers to common questions.

I am not good with computers.

Each role sees a simple screen with only its own tools, and short guides are provided in English and Bangla.

What if the internet goes down?

The POS and kitchen screen keep running. Cash orders are saved and sync when the connection returns.

Do I have more than one branch?

Branches are built in. Managers see their own branch and you see them all, with profit side by side.

Can I take bKash and Nagad?

Yes: cash, bKash, Nagad and card, including split payments across methods.

Do I need the website?

Not on day one. Start with the till and kitchen, and turn on online ordering when you want it.

Whose data is it?

Yours. Every client has a separate installation and database, with daily backups.

Can I connect delivery apps?

Orders from any delivery app can be entered on a built-in screen from day one. Direct connections depend on each platform's partner access.

What about VAT and receipts?

VAT and service charge are configurable per branch. Please confirm local requirements with your accountant.
