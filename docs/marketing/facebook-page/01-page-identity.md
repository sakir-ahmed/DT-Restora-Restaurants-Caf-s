- Template: **Business** (or "Services").
- Tab order: Home, Posts, Services, Reviews, Photos, Videos, About, Events.
- Turn on: Messenger, Reviews, Services. Turn off: Shop, Offers (unless you use them).
- Featured section: pin the launch offer post, the free demo video and your best customer story when you have one.

## 8. Roles and security (do this on day one)

- Add at least **two admins** (you and a trusted colleague), so the page is never locked.
- Turn on **two-factor authentication** for every admin.
- Use **Meta Business Suite** to schedule posts and read messages.
- Do not share the personal Facebook login. Give access through Page roles.
