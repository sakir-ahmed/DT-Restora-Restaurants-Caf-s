**English**

DT Restora is a complete restaurant and cafe management system made by Datatech BD Ltd in Dhaka.

Most restaurants lose money in small ways nobody sees: waste that is never written down, stock that is a guess, cash that does not match and profit that is only known at month end. DT Restora makes every one of those leaks visible.

What you get in one system:

- POS with tables, hold orders, split bills, cash, bKash, Nagad and card
- Live kitchen display for counter, QR table and website orders
- Recipe costing, automatic stock deduction, purchasing and wastage log
- Staff attendance (fingerprint device supported), payroll and salary slips
- Loyalty points, coupons and SMS offers
- Your own bilingual ordering website, QR table ordering, delivery and live tracking
- Delivery-app orders with commission recorded
- Automatic accounting, owner dashboard, branch comparison and reports
- Works offline: the POS and kitchen screen keep running when the internet drops

Launch offer: 1,000 BDT per month (regular price 5,700 BDT). One-time installation 2,500 BDT, refundable after one year of use if you cancel. First month free after installation. Yearly plan: 2 months free.
