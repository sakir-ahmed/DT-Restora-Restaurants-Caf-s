## 4. Stories, Reels and live

- **Stories (daily, 2 minutes):** reshare the day's post, add a poll sticker, or show a 15-second screen recording of one feature. Use `../social-media-posts/brand-assets/story-*.png` as backgrounds.
- **Reels (2 per week):** build from carousel images (see the video script inside each carousel's `post.txt`). Vertical, 15 to 30 seconds, captions on.
- **Live demo (weekly):** 30 minutes, same day and time each week. Create a Facebook Event with `assets/event-cover-free-demo.png`, share it two days before and answer questions live. Save the video to the page.
- **Customer story (when you have one):** a short quote or photo from a live restaurant. Ask for permission in writing first.

## 6. Success measures for the first month (check on Sundays)

- Messages received and how many turned into a booked demo.
- Page follows and reach (watch the trend, not one number).
- Best three posts by comments and saves, then make more like them.
- Leads per post: ask each new contact which post or video they saw and record the post id (for example P012) in the lead tracker. Full tracking is in `../social-media-posts/content-plan.json`.
