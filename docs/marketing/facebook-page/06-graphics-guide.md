## Uploading

1. **Profile picture:** Page, Edit profile picture. Upload the 1080 square. Facebook shows it as a circle, about 170 px on desktop and 128 px on phones.
2. **Cover:** Edit cover photo, upload at 1640x624. The cover is shown at about 820x312 on desktop and about 640x360 on phones, so phones crop the left and right edges.
3. After uploading, use Facebook's **preview** for desktop and mobile and drag only if needed.

## Safe zones (already built in)

- All covers keep the **logo, headline and sub-line in the centre column** (the middle 896 px of 1640). That column stays visible on both desktop and phone.
- The two tilted screenshots at the edges are decoration, so it does not matter if phones crop them.
- On desktop the **profile picture overlaps the bottom-left** of the cover. Nothing important is placed there.

## Changing text or screenshots

The graphics are generated by a script. To change them:

1. Edit `../social/_source/fbpage.py` (texts, colours, screenshot choices).
2. Run `python3 docs/marketing/social/_source/fbpage.py`.
3. Run `node docs/marketing/social/_source/render_fbpage.mjs` (uses the project's Playwright install).

## Tips

- Change the cover every 4 to 6 weeks (offer, season, new feature) so returning visitors see something new.
- Use the Bangla cover only if most of your audience prefers Bangla. A good compromise is the English cover with a Bangla pinned post.
- Keep screenshots current. If the product screens change, re-capture and re-run the scripts.
- Always show the **free demo link** on the cover or in the first line of the pinned post.
