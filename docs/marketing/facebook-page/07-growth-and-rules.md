# DT Restora Facebook page: growth, groups, ads and rules

## 1. Content mix

| Share | Type | Examples in `../social-media-posts/posts/` |
|---|---|---|
| 30% | Education and tips | `04-owner-tips-seo`, `13-mini-infographics` |
| 25% | Feature spotlights | `01-feature-spotlights`, `05-carousels-video` |
| 20% | Owner problems, questions, myths | `02-problem-solution`, `09-quick-answers`, `11-myth-vs-fact`, `12-owner-chat` |
| 15% | Offers and demo calls | `03-offers-pricing` |
| 10% | Engagement | `07-engagement`, `10-quiz-and-polls` |

Language mix: about 80% English and 20% Bangla at the start. If Bangla posts get more messages, raise the Bangla share.

## 2. Facebook groups (the best free source of leads)

- Search for groups of restaurant owners, cafe owners, food entrepreneurs, e-commerce food businesses and restaurant staff in Bangladesh.
- Join 5 to 10. Read the rules. Many groups ban direct promotion.
- For the first week, only **comment helpfully** (answer questions about stock, waste, staff, cost). Link nothing.
- Then post one value post per group per week (a tip or an infographic), not an advert. Mention the page only in your profile or when asked.
- Reply to anyone who asks, in Messenger, with the saved replies.

## 3. Partnerships

- Restaurant equipment suppliers and kitchen designers
- Interior designers and restaurant consultants
- Accountants and auditors serving food businesses
- Food delivery photographers and menu designers
- Cafe and restaurant owner associations

Offer each one a short pitch, the demo link and a tracked link (use a different `utm_campaign` value per partner).

## 4. Facebook ads (when organic posts are working)

Start small and learn.

- **Budget:** about 5,000 BDT per week to test, as in `../social-media-posts/campaign/100-sales-plan-90-days.md`.
- **Objective:** Messages (to Messenger or WhatsApp) or Leads.
- **Audience:** restaurant, cafe and food-business owners and managers in your target areas, aged about 25 to 55. Try interests such as restaurant management, food business and cafe owners.
- **Creatives:** use `../social-media-posts/brand-assets/ad-sq-*.png` and `ad-wide-*.png`, plus the best organic posts.
- **Text:** the first two lines of the matching `post.txt`.
- **Landing:** a Messenger conversation, WhatsApp chat or the demo link with a tracked URL.
- **Judge after 7 days:** cost per message, messages that become demos, cost per demo.
- **Policy:** Meta may need a privacy policy link and may restrict claims. Avoid "guaranteed", before-and-after income claims and personal attributes.

## 5. Posting rules of thumb

- Every post has one clear idea and one next step (comment, message, demo link).
- Put the key line in the first 125 characters, before "See more".
- Answer every comment and message. Reply to a question with another question to keep the conversation going.
- Ask people to **comment** (it helps reach), but do not use "like and share to win" tricks.
- Post consistently (once a day or at least 4 times a week) rather than in bursts.
- Reuse content: turn each carousel into a Reel, each tip into a story, each quiz into a poll.

## 6. SEO for the Facebook page

- Use the key words in the page name, intro, About text and post captions (see `01-page-identity.md`).
- Fill every field of the page: category, services, website, hours and contact. Complete pages rank better.
- Link the page from your website footer and your Google Business Profile.
- Use consistent name and handle across Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn and YouTube.
- Use clear alt text on photos (it is generated in each `post.txt`).
- Get genuine reviews: ask every happy customer (message in `03-messenger-and-replies.md`).

## 7. Do and do not

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Show the real product and be clear about what it does | Promise income or fixed savings |
| Explain the offer exactly: 1,000 BDT per month, 2,500 BDT installation, first month free | Run fake countdowns or "only 3 seats left" |
| Credit customers and ask permission for their stories | Use a restaurant's name or photos without permission |
| Say plainly that bKash, Nagad, card payments, SMS and website orders need internet | Claim it works offline for everything |
| Reply politely to criticism and fix it | Delete honest negative reviews |

## 8. Monthly review

Every month write down:

1. Messages, demos booked, demos held and customers won from Facebook.
2. The three best and three worst posts and why.
3. One change for next month (new format, new time, new audience).
4. Update the cover and pinned post if the offer changed.
