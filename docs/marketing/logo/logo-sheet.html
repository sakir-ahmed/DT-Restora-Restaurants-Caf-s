Why this mark

The cloche

The silver serving dome is the universal sign of a restaurant kitchen and good service. A guest, a cook and an owner all recognise it at once, and it is friendly rather than technical.

The rising bars

Three bars cut into the dome climb from left to right: orders, control, profit. Lift the lid and you find growth. That is the one idea the owner is buying, a business that grows when it can see its numbers.

Slate and amber

Deep slate says trust, money and calm control. Amber is warm like a kitchen light and a golden crust, and it is the same pair the DT Restora app already uses, so the brand and the product look like one family.

Small sizes

The mark stays readable down to 16 px, so it works as a favicon, a phone icon and a WhatsApp profile picture.

Usage

Keep clear space equal to the height of the dome knob on every side. Never stretch, rotate or recolour the mark. On photos use the reversed or white monochrome version. For one colour printing, stamps and receipts use the black monochrome version.
