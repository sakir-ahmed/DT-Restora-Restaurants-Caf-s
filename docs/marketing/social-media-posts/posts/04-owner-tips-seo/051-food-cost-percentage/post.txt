How to calculate food cost percentage

Food cost percentage is the cost of ingredients in a dish divided by its selling price, times 100. Check it dish by dish, because averages hide the items that quietly lose money. A recipe-linked system keeps the numbers current as prices change.

- Add up what a dish costs to make
- Divide by the selling price
- Multiply by 100
- Compare dish by dish
- Reprice the worst performers
