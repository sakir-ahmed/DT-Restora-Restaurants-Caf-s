Cash handling rules that work

Good cash control protects both the owner and the cashier. One drawer per shift, counted at open and close, with approvals and a visible log, removes doubt before it becomes a dispute.

- One cashier per drawer per shift
- Count at open and close
- Approve discounts above a limit
- Record every void with a reason
- Review the activity log weekly
