5 ways to reduce food waste

Waste drops when it is measured. Start by logging every loss with a reason, then review the pattern each week. Most kitchens find two or three repeat causes that are easy to fix once they are visible.

- Count stock on a schedule
- Cook to forecast, not to fear
- Use first-in, first-out
- Review waste weekly
