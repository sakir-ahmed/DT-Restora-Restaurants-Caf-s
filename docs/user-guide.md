# DT Restora user guide

This guide is for the people who run a restaurant on DT Restora: the owner, branch manager, cashier, kitchen, accountant, and rider.

## Sign in

Open the staff login page. Sign in with your username, email, or mobile and password. The menu shows only the jobs your role is allowed to do. Switch the language between English and Bangla from the top bar.

## Owner

The dashboard shows sales, food cost, wastage, salary, expenses, and net profit. The same totals appear in Reports and in the accounting profit and loss statement. Use Settings for the discount limit, wastage alert, POS lock time, and delivery OTP. The activity log records who changed what. The customer directory lists people who have ordered and their loyalty points.

## Cashier

Open a shift and count the opening cash. Take orders from the POS, hold a ticket if the guest is still deciding, then take cash or a recorded wallet payment. After a few idle minutes the POS locks and asks for your staff PIN. The open ticket stays on the screen. If the network drops, the order is saved on the device and syncs when the connection returns. Close the shift by counting the drawer. The screen shows the expected cash and the difference.

## Kitchen

The kitchen display lists new tickets. Mark them preparing, ready, then served. Log wastage with a reason. The cost is calculated from the ingredient price. Stock counts record what is on the shelf and post the difference.

## Accountant

Accounting posts sales, purchases, wastage, salary, and vouchers. Use the ledger, cash flow, payables aging, and bank or wallet matching. Account names can be renamed. The codes used by automatic postings stay in place. Lock a fiscal year when the period is closed.

## Customer website

Guests browse the menu, open an item, and check out for delivery or pickup. They can sign in with a mobile code or Google. The tracking page shows the rider on the map when a location has been shared. From the account page a customer can ask for their personal data to be removed. Orders stay for the restaurant's records, with the name and mobile cleared.

## Rider

The rider screen lists assigned deliveries. Share location while on a trip. For cash on delivery, enter the customer's code when that check is turned on, then mark the delivery complete.
